{{app}} keeps your snippets, your notes and your lists within reach of the keyboard. Everything stays on this machine, encrypted; nothing goes over the network.

## Where things are
- **Notes**, **Tools** and **HTTP**: the three areas, at the side (Ctrl+1, Ctrl+2, Ctrl+3).
- Cards file themselves: pinned, today, this week, older. The **Board** view, at the top, shows folders as zones.
- The **About** menu holds the getting-started guide and every shortcut.

## The keys that matter
| Keys | For |
| --- | --- |
| Ctrl+K | Search the notes, or the tools |
| Ctrl+N · Ctrl+Shift+N | A snippet · a Note |
| Enter · C · P | Open · copy · pin the card |
| Ctrl+Alt+P | Paste a note from any application |

These samples are made to be edited, or sent to the trash.
